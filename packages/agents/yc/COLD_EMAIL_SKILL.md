# Cold Email Writer

Candidate defaults:
- Name: Yash
- Portfolio: https://yashworks.com
- Resume: https://yashworks.com/resume
- Email: contact@yashworks.com

## When to Use

- Cold outreach to a hiring manager, founder, or recruiter
- Job opening with no referral
- Introducing yourself to a company you want to work at
- Mentions: cold email, reach out, intro email, outreach, contact hiring manager

## Core Principle

Cold emails fail because they sound like cover letters. Sound like a smart person who genuinely noticed something specific about the company - not a candidate broadcasting credentials.

The email should feel like: a colleague mentioning something interesting they read, not a sales pitch.

## Template

### Subject
```
Quick intro - Yash
```

### Opening - The Hook (2-3 sentences)
Specific to this company. Not a generic compliment.

Good: a stat, product decision, market insight, or technical angle from their site/JD.
Bad: "I love what you're building" / "Your mission resonates" / restating their About page as "{Company} is a ... company that builds X".

### Location
Never mention country, city, timezone, or "based in". Do not disclose location.

### Experience Gap (if applicable)
Acknowledge briefly, redirect to shipped work. Do not apologize.

### Body shape
1. Hi <Name>,
2. One short opener sentence (specific to company)
3. Exactly 4-5 bullet points - that is the email
4. Portfolio line + sign-off

No essays. No section labels inside Body.

### Portfolio
```
More about me: https://yashworks.com
```

### Closing
```
Yash
contact@yashworks.com
```

## Length
One opener sentence + 4-5 bullets. Keep it short.

## Research First
1. Company angle from website/JD scrape - one specific hook
2. Recipient name when possible
3. Relevant projects from resume
4. Stack overlap only if real

## Avoid
- Location / "based in" / country / city
- Inventing projects or domain experience not on the resume
- Forcing industry cosplay when resume is generalist engineering (APIs/full-stack is valid everywhere)
- Em/en dashes (use ASCII hyphen or rewrite)
- "I look forward to hearing from you"
- "Please find attached my resume"
- "I would love the opportunity..."
- "exceptional addition" / "uniquely positions me"
